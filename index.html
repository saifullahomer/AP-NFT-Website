Do You Have Questions?

We’ve Got Your Answers.

Whether you’re finding your signature scent or picking a gift, we’re here to help. Explore answers about perfumes, orders, and samples — for a smooth, delightful experience.

How long does the scent typically last?

Our eau de parfum is designed to linger for 6–8 hours. Lasting power varies with your skin, the fragrance, and the weather. Apply to pulse points for the best experience.

How can I make the order?

Explore the catalog, select your favorite fragrance, and add it to your bag. This storefront is a preview; payments and order fulfillment will be connected before launch.

Do you offer samples or discovery sets?

A little exploration is the perfect way to find your scent. Contact us to learn about discovery sets and available samples.

Are your ingredients cruelty-free and ethical?

Our philosophy is rooted in thoughtfully sourced ingredients and care for the world around us. Contact us for ingredient and sourcing details for each fragrance.
